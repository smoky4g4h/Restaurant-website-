OUR STORY

A journey of flavors, passion, and culinary excellence.

Welcome to Savory & Co., where tradition meets modern innovation. Established in 2024, we set out with a simple mission: to create an unforgettable dining experience that celebrates the rich heritage of Indian cuisine while embracing contemporary techniques.

Our chefs meticulously source the finest ingredients, from saffron hand-picked in Kashmir to spices ground fresh daily in our kitchen. Every dish is a labor of love, designed to transport you to a world of exquisite flavors.
